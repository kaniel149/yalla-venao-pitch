Launch Roadmap

What's Left — The Launch Process

~10 working days from code to live orders.

1

Backend & Auth

Connect Supabase — database, authentication, real-time subscriptions.

  • Supabase project setup
  • User auth (email/phone)
  • Orders table + real-time
  • RLS security policies

2-3 days

2

Vendor Onboarding

Sign up the 13 businesses — real menus, real photos, signed agreements.

  • Visit each business in person
  • Photograph dishes & venue
  • Collect real menus & prices
  • Sign vendor agreements
  • Replace Unsplash stock photos

2-3 days (field work)

3

WhatsApp & Payments

Order flow: customer orders → vendor gets WhatsApp → courier delivers → cash.

  • WhatsApp Business API for order alerts
  • Yappy integration (Panama payments)
  • Cash on delivery flow
  • Order status push notifications

2-3 days

4

Courier Recruitment

Recruit 3-5 local riders. Scooter/bike. Train the app.

  • Post courier-signup landing page
  • WhatsApp group for couriers
  • Training on courier portal
  • Set delivery zones & fees

2-3 days

5

Deploy & Test

Go live on production domain. Test the full order cycle end-to-end.

  • Domain: yallavenao.com
  • Vercel production deploy
  • 5 test orders with real businesses
  • Fix edge cases

1 day

6

Launch & Marketing

Ads go live. Instagram launch. Word of mouth takes over.

  • Instagram Reels: "order from the hammock"
  • Meta ads: $30-45/day geo-targeted
  • Flyers at hostels & surf camps
  • Launch party at Wao Beach Bar
